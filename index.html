Frequently asked

Do I have to subscribe? No. The first seven days are free. Afterwards, nudge pro is needed only to add new tasks. Everything you already have stays fully editable.

Where are my tasks stored? On your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud database. Nobody else can see them.

What does the email import do? Connect your Gmail, Outlook, iCloud Mail or any IMAP mailbox in Settings, then send yourself an email whenever something comes to mind. nudge turns emails you send to yourself into tasks: the subject becomes the title, the text becomes notes. When the same note arrives again, it joins the task you already have and moves it to the top of the board. nudge only reads your inbox; your mailbox is never changed. Gmail and Outlook are connected by signing in with Google or Microsoft with read-only access.
