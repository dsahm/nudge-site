Privacy Policy

smart nudges for iPhone and iPad · Effective 25 September 2026

smart nudges is designed so that your data stays yours. The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Tasks and notes

Your tasks, notes, tags, reminders and attachments are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled in the app's settings, they are also stored in the private database of your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit service, so that your own devices can share them. The developer has no access to this data.

Email import

If you connect a mailbox, smart nudges reads your inbox directly from your device. Only emails sent from your own address to your own address are turned into tasks: the subject becomes the title, the text becomes notes, and files attached to those emails are copied into the app's own storage as task files, the same way as files you attach to a task yourself. If iCloud sync is enabled, those files are also stored in the private database of your iCloud account. Small images that the message body embeds, such as signature logos, are skipped. smart nudges never marks, moves, deletes or sends emails, and no email content, address or credential is ever sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

Gmail and Outlook. These mailboxes are connected by signing in with your Google or Microsoft account in a browser window. smart nudges only asks for read access (Google: gmail.readonly; Microsoft: Mail.Read and User.Read) and receives an access token, never your password. The token is stored in the iOS keychain on your device and, when iCloud Keychain is enabled in iOS, synchronised by Apple between your devices. Disconnecting the mailbox in smart nudges deletes the token; you can also revoke smart nudges' access at any time in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

iCloud Mail and other IMAP servers. These are read over an encrypted IMAP connection with your email address, server details and an app-specific password or password, stored in the iOS keychain in the same way.

Google API Services User Data Policy. smart nudges' use of information received from Gmail APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show you the emails you sent to yourself as tasks inside smart nudges. It is not used for advertising, is never sold, is never transferred to humans or to other parties, and is never used to train models. Gmail data stays on your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in the private database of your iCloud account.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No notification content leaves the device.

Purchases

The nudge pro subscription is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app receives only a confirmation of the subscription status. It does not receive your name, address or payment details.

Photos and files

Photos or files you attach to a task are copied into the app's own storage and, if iCloud sync is on, into your private iCloud database. The app only accesses the items you pick.

Data you control

Deleting a task deletes it from your devices and your iCloud database. Turning iCloud sync off keeps data on the device only. Deleting the app removes all local data; your iCloud data stays until you delete it in the app or through iCloud settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Questions can be raised at github.com/dsahm/nudge-site/issues.
