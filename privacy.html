Privacy Policy

nudge for iPhone and iPad · Effective 19 September 2026

nudge is designed so that your data stays yours. The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Tasks and notes

Your tasks, notes, tags, reminders and attachments are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled in the app's settings, they are also stored in the private database of your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit service, so that your own devices can share them. The developer has no access to this data.

Email import

If you connect a mailbox, nudge reads your inbox over an encrypted IMAP connection directly from your device to your email provider. Only emails sent from your own address to your own address are turned into tasks. nudge never marks, moves, deletes or sends emails. Your email address, server details and password are stored in the iOS keychain on your device and, when iCloud Keychain is enabled in iOS, synchronised by Apple between your devices. They are never sent anywhere else.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No notification content leaves the device.

Purchases

The nudge pro subscription is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app receives only a confirmation of the subscription status. It does not receive your name, address or payment details.

Photos and files

Photos or files you attach to a task are copied into the app's own storage and, if iCloud sync is on, into your private iCloud database. The app only accesses the items you pick.

Data you control

Deleting a task deletes it from your devices and your iCloud database. Turning iCloud sync off keeps data on the device only. Deleting the app removes all local data; your iCloud data stays until you delete it in the app or through iCloud settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Questions can be raised at github.com/dsahm/nudge-site/issues.
